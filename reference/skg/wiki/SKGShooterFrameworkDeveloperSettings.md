# FirearmCollision:
### Firearm Collision Channel:
The collision/trace channel to be used for firearm collision detection. I always recommend making a dedicated channel just for this purpose.
# LightLaser:
### Laser Collision Channel:
The collision/trace channel to be used for Laser end detection. I always recommend making a dedicated channel just for this purpose.
# RangeFinder:
### Range Finder Collision Channel:
The collision/trace channel to be used for Laser of the range finder for end detection. I always recommend making a dedicated channel just for this purpose.
